About ElRay

Solar shouldn't mean guesswork.

Designed from a real inspection, powered by the sun.

I started ElRay Energy out of a real interest in renewable energy, and a conviction that solar in Nigeria doesn't have to mean guesswork, inflated quotes, or a system that quietly fails the first time it's tested.

Every ElRay install also means less reliance on generators, cleaner, quieter, and cheaper to run over time.

Too many homeowners and business owners get a number over the phone, pay a deposit, and find out months later that the system can't actually carry what they need it to. That gap between what's promised and what's delivered is the problem ElRay was built to close.

ElRay is built on one rule: we inspect before we quote. That's slower than a phone-call estimate, but it means the system we design is the system your home or business actually needs, and it means you can trust the number on the page.

We're based in Mowe, Ogun State, and currently focused on doing a small number of installations properly rather than a large number quickly. Every job is an opportunity to prove the audit-first approach works, and to build the kind of reputation that comes from systems that hold up.

— The ElRay Energy team
