Our Process

From first visit to a system that's genuinely yours.

Five steps, in order; this is the actual sequence every ElRay project follows. No step is skipped to hit a deadline.

01

Site Survey

An ElRay engineer visits your property to assess your existing electrical load, wiring condition, available roof or ground space, and day-to-day usage patterns. This is a physical visit, not a phone call.

02

Load Assessment & Design

Survey data is translated into a system spec; panel count, inverter size, and battery capacity calculated for your real load, not a standard package. This is where the audit becomes a design.

03

Transparent Quote

You receive a quote tied directly to the audit; what's specified, why it's sized that way, and what it costs. Every line traces back to something we measured on-site.

04

Installation

Our technicians install to spec, with a clear timeline agreed before work begins, so you know what to expect and when.

05

Handover & Support

You get full documentation of what was installed and how it was sized, plus a direct line to ElRay after the system goes live.

Audited. Documented. Yours.
