Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

ElRay Energy Ltd. ("ElRay," "we," "us") provides audited solar installation and maintenance services in Mowe, Ogun State, Nigeria and environs. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

Information you give us directly

When you submit our site survey request form, we collect your full name, phone number, property address, property type, and any details you choose to add about what you're looking to power. Email is optional. This information is submitted through our form and processed by Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, before reaching our team.

Information collected automatically

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site, pages viewed, general location at a country/region level, and device type. We do not use this to identify you individually.

We also plan to use Google Ads, which again, only with your consent, may use cookies to measure ad performance and show relevant ads based on your visit to this site.

You control this consent through the banner shown when you first visit, and your choice is remembered for future visits. You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site, or by adjusting cookie settings in your browser.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your site survey request and schedule an inspection
  • To communicate with you about your enquiry or an active project
  • To understand site usage and improve the website, where you've consented to analytics
  • To measure and improve advertising performance, where you've consented and once we begin running ads

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Who we share information with

  • Formspree - processes contact form submissions on our behalf
  • Google (Analytics and, in future, Ads) - receives usage and cookie data only where you've given consent

We don't share your information with any other third party except where required by law.

How long we keep your information

We retain site survey submissions for 12 months from your last contact with us, unless you ask us to delete it sooner, or a longer period is required to comply with a legal obligation.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Object to or restrict how we process your information
  • Withdraw consent for analytics or advertising cookies at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. If you're not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Cookies

This site uses cookies only for analytics and advertising, and only after you consent via the banner shown on your first visit. We don't use cookies for any purpose beyond what's described in this policy.

Contact us

For any question about this policy or your information, contact us at elrayenergy@gmail.com or via the details on our Contact page.
